About me

Forest ecologist, quantitative modeller, and curious naturalist.

I am an Associate Professor of Forest Ecology at Isparta University of Applied Sciences (Türkiye) and a TÜBİTAK-funded postdoctoral researcher at KU Leuven’s Division of Forest, Nature & Landscape. My work lies at the intersection of forest ecology, biogeography, and biodiversity modelling.

I am particularly interested in how functional traits, community assembly, and disturbance regimes (especially fire) interact to shape Mediterranean and Anatolian ecosystems. I combine empirical data, large trait databases and advanced R-based workflows.

Research & projects

Selected thematic lines and ongoing collaborations.

Mediterranean treelines & climate change

Trait-based and SDM approaches to understand how treeline species respond to changing climate, focusing on Abies cilicica and other key taxa in the Taurus and Levant mountain systems.

  • Species distribution modelling (CHELSA, CMIP scenarios)
  • Functional traits and demographic bottlenecks
  • Collaboration with EFI & Velux Stiftung consortium

Functional diversity of bird & mammal communities

Understanding how fire and land-use mosaics shape trait space occupancy, specialization and redundancy in Mediterranean assemblages.

  • Camera-trap & point-count data
  • FRic, FEve, FDiv, FDis, RaoQ, CWMs
  • Post-fire community reassembly in SW Türkiye

New metrics for ecosystem “naturalness”

Developing and testing Benford-, fractal- and entropy-based indices to assess ecological integrity and deviations from natural reference conditions.

  • Deng & Kaniadakis entropy in niche analyses
  • Fractal geometry of forest patches
  • Applications to earthworm & forest datasets

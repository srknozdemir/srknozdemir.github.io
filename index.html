About me

Forest ecologist, quantitative modeller, and curious naturalist.

I am an Associate Professor of Forest Ecology at Isparta University of Applied Sciences (Türkiye), where my research bridges forest ecology, biogeography, and quantitative biodiversity science. I previously held a TÜBİTAK-funded postdoctoral fellowship at KU Leuven's Division of Forest, Nature & Landscape (Belgium), an experience that continues to shape my international, interdisciplinary outlook and ongoing collaborations.

My research investigates how functional traits, community assembly, and disturbance regimes—particularly fire—interact to structure Mediterranean and Anatolian ecosystems under accelerating climate change. By integrating field-based empirical data, large trait and occurrence databases, and reproducible R-based modelling workflows, I work to translate ecological pattern into mechanistic understanding and actionable conservation insight.

Selected thematic lines and ongoing collaborations.

Mediterranean treelines & climate refugia

Integrative niche modelling to map the climatic tolerance and refugia of treeline species such as Abies cilicica under future climate scenarios.

Functional diversity of bird & mammal communities

Understanding how fire and land-use mosaics shape trait space occupancy, specialization and redundancy in Mediterranean assemblages.

Diplodia sapinea risk in black pine forests

Assessing the prevalence, damage severity, and molecular confirmation of Diplodia shoot dieback in Anatolian black pine (Pinus nigra), and modelling its distribution risk under current and future climate scenarios.

Diplodia sapineaPinus nigraForest pathologySDM

LAMP detection of plane tree canker

Using LAMP technology for the first time in Türkiye to rapidly detect Ceratocystis platani in plane trees, mapping its spread with GIS and training stakeholders in biosecurity practices.

Ceratocystis plataniLAMPBiosecurityGIS

New metrics for ecosystem naturalness

Developing Benford-, fractal- and entropy-based indices to assess ecological integrity and deviations from natural reference conditions.
